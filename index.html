About Chaffee Provides

Purchasing from local producers helps keep Chaffee County working lands intact. Productive agricultural land preserves stunning views, water quality and wildlife habitat, and protects our valuable lands from over-development. Agriculture is Chaffee County's fifth-largest economic sector, and it exists and endures because of neighbors who work these lands.

Increasing Food Security

Chaffee Provides launched during the COVID-19 pandemic, which revealed vulnerabilities in our food systems.

Local producers stepped in to fill the gaps as residents became more interested in supporting local foodsheds. Chaffee County Community Foundation’s Food Access Cohort created this site to increase food system coordination and ensure Chaffee County residents’ access to nutritious food, and continues with the support of Chaffee Common Ground and Guidestone Colorado.

Guidestone's mission is to grow a vibrant agricultural future through education, community building, and partnerships. The organization's strategies are founded on the belief that meaningful exposure and experiences on local farms and ranches raises awareness and appreciation, and leads to choices that support and increase food system capacity. The result of these choices will be resilient local food systems, healthier communities, and a vibrant future for local agriculture.

We hope you enjoy exploring the bounty of food local agriculture provides while keeping working lands working.
